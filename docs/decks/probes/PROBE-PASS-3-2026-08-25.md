## §B The lighthouse: a fit-out that did not run, and nothing said so

The document asked `building.grammar@0` with `archetype: "lighthouse",
floors: 2, roof: "flat"`. The catalog's entry (`stdlib catalog.ts`,
implemented, wave 6) promises *"the beacon spire, maritime: white-and-red
terracotta bands from plinth to plate, a solid gallery deck with a parapet
over the plate, and a sea-lantern"*, and `archetypes-terminus.ts` has
`fitLighthouse` for it. The world holds the plain shell — a two-floor box
under the flat roof the author asked for — and the compile carries no
diagnostic that the lighthouse's own fit-out was skipped or overruled. The
model's `floors: 2` is F27's scale problem again (a lighthouse asked for
at house height); the silence is the compiler's, and a new cause:

- **F29 — an archetype's bespoke fit-out is defeated by the author's
  params (or falls back) without a diagnostic.** The catalog says spire and
  lantern; the world says box; the author hears nothing. The decay engine
  already has this shape of note (`LOAM-W511 DECAY_MODE_FALLBACK`); the
  fit-out needs its sibling, and the probe that finds *why* `fitLighthouse`
  gave a box (the `roof: "flat"` override? a floors floor?) is the next
  unit.

## §D Unit 34 — F29 answered: the silent fit-out speaks

The lighthouse's own report meta says why: `wallTop` 16, a flat `roofTop`
17, `ROOF_FLOURISH_RISE` 1 — the rebuild's plan has base 17 and top 18, one
course where `roofPlan` demands two, so the plan is null and
`fitLighthouse` runs only its keeper's-room furniture; the bands, the
gallery and the sea-lantern live inside `if (plan !== null)`. And
`roofPlan` is not one function: 25 archetype files carry a private copy
(the census's class 3, row 3.4), and about 170 `if (plan !== null)` sites
skip their signature work on a null plan with no channel to say so —
`FitOutContext` had none.

Landed (payload-identical; nothing moves a block):

- `FitOutContext.skipped?: string[]` — a sink a fit-out pushes one line
  per skipped thing into; threaded from `core.ts` through `FurnishRequest`
  and surfaced as `meta.fitOutSkipped` on the built shell.
- Every `roofPlan` copy names its reason before returning null (the
  terminus copy by hand, the other 24 by an opus-5-low batch,
  `scratchpad/f29/ROOFPLAN-COPIES.md`).
- `LOAM-W524 FITOUT_ROOF_SKIPPED` from `structures/buildings.ts`: on the
  pass-3 document it reads *"lighthouse" skipped the work that makes it
  itself — roof work: 1 course above the eave where the rebuild needs 2 —
  a flat or low roof leaves no room — and the plain shell stands in its
  place*, with the fix hint the compile-feedback rounds need (drop `roof:
  "flat"` or add a storey).
- `stdlib/test/fitout-skipped.test.ts`: the lighthouse at `roof: "flat"`
  names the missing course; with a roof that has room, nothing is skipped.

The roof itself is not overridden (D75): a flat roof is the author's
request, the feedback rounds are where it changes, and the warning is what
they were missing. F29 closes; the 25 copies are the census's to
consolidate.
