# F27 — "the centerpiece dominates" goes into the kit

Stocktake Run, unit 31 (2026-08-25). Pre-registered in REACH before the
spend. The kit's icon register (`settlement-author.md` §9e) taught
saturation — "an icon is a cheap plugin program … small envelopes,
double-digit counts" — and nothing about the one icon the prompt names.
Probe pass 2 showed the result: a bell pavilion at 1.27× the houses, a
ferry rig lower than a shed, pit heads that are huts. The change adds one
paragraph, **the centerpiece dominates** — a landmark ≥ 1.5× the ordinary
buildings' height and ≥ 2× their footprint, or standing above them, with
the brief and `envelope` saying so — and one sentence on the `envelope`
row. `settlement-core.md` and the modules regenerated by `split-kit.mjs`;
the kit's own suites pass (13 files, 267 tests).

## §C What is left, and whose it is

- **F28 — the icon metric's dominance rule.** `dominant` requires height
  ≥ 1.5× *and* footprint ≥ 2× the ordinary buildings. A rope ferry across a
  fjord, a mine working, a monastery on a peak, a bell pavilion on a ridge
  are dominant by span or elevation and can never be by height. Four reads
  now disagree with the rule on the same worlds (law 7: a metric that stops
  agreeing with reads is retired, not optimised). The rule needs
  *prominence* — elevation of the icon's base above the buildings' median
  base, and span — beside height. A metric unit, with these four as its
  evidence.
- **F27's remainder** — the bell and the mine — is what the teaching did
  not reach on two documents: the model widened the pavilion and dug a
  working, and neither reads. Whether the paragraph needs a second sentence
  ("if it cannot be tall, put it above the roofs or make it the biggest
  thing in view") or these two need a second roll is a question for probe
  pass 3, which runs six *new* prompts for G4 anyway.

## §D Verdict

The kit change **ships**: the golden gate is green and no golden icon
regressed; two of the four probes it was written for now read as their
prompts. The metric is now the thing standing between the reads and the
verdicts (F28).
