## §B The delta: a settlement the grammar cannot seat

The prompt asked for houses on tall stilts above the flood. The document
has `stilt_house_*` buildings and a `confluence_floating_market` — and
two `plateau` edits that raise dry shelves under them, because nothing in
the kit or the compiler seats a building over water: the kit's seating
law is "how a grounded thing meets the ground", the pad contract lowers
or raises *ground*, and a footprint on water is refused or reseated. The
model did the only thing it could and built land. Nothing failed loudly;
the world is a delta village on two islands, not a stilt village.

- **F30 — no seat over water.** A building (or a market) whose pad falls
  on water has no way to stand on piles to the bed; the author routes
  around it with terrain. A feature, not a fix: **P8** — a `seat: "piles"`
  (or a water-pad claim class) that drives piles from the footprint to the
  bed and floors the building at the requested height above the water,
  with the pad contract's `waterFloor` rule as its floor. Kit-teaching
  follows the feature.
