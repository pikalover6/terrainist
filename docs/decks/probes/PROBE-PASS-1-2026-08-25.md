## §B What the pass could not judge, and why

The golden harness is **authoring-only** by design (README: "no bespoke
programs … three quarters of the bill"). Five of the six probes reach for
an `authored:<id>` program for the very thing the prompt screams — the
ferry rig, the bell pavilion, the ribcage market and skull temple, the pit
heads and smelting battery, the prayer-flag span — and in this mode those
programs are references with no body: the compiler drops them (`W337`),
the icon is *present* at document level and absent from the world, and
dominance reads "nothing placed to measure" or measures a bystander. The
one probe with no program-carried icon, the caldera, has nothing to be
dominant over.

So the instrument's verdict on pass 1 is a verdict on *authoring reach*
(6/6, four new packs, no box, no era violation) and not on T1/T8. That is
the pass's one new failure class, and it is the method's: **F24 — probe
prompts must run the program stage**. Pass 2 runs the same six through the
full pipeline (`record-generate-run.mjs`, programs authored, ≈ 3× the
cost) before any of these six count toward G4.
